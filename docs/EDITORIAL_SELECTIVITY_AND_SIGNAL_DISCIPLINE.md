# Editorial Selectivity & Signal Discipline

BeyondFleet should help readers pay attention to fewer things, more
thoughtfully.

The platform should feel curated because it is comfortable omitting noise.

## Core Standard

Before surfacing a brief, recommendation, continuity cue, or topic, ask:

- Does this actually matter?
- Does it change an assumption?
- Does it affect long-term thinking?
- Does it deserve follow-through?
- Is this a signal, or temporary noise?

If the answer is weak, do less.

## Daily Brief Selection

Not every macro event deserves a Daily Brief.

Surface a Daily Brief when the signal:

- changes a prior assumption
- clarifies a risk condition
- connects to a durable learning theme
- deserves revisiting after a future event
- helps the reader think more clearly this week

Keep it quiet when the signal:

- is only a price move
- is already widely absorbed
- does not alter the working question
- requires too much forced interpretation
- exists mainly because the system needs content

Sometimes the best editorial line is:

```text
Not much changed. Wait for better evidence.
```

## Anti-Overinterpretation

Avoid:

- forced narratives
- exaggerated second-order effects
- artificial macro importance
- performative nuance
- intellectual inflation
- turning every data point into a regime shift

Good second-order analysis is earned by the evidence. If the evidence is thin,
write a shorter note.

## Recommendation Minimalism

Recommendations should feel rare enough to matter.

Use:

- zero to two related lessons
- zero recommendations when the fit is weak
- one continuation cue when continuity is meaningful
- no trending topics when there is no real recurrence

The absence of a recommendation can itself be editorial judgment.

## Selective Continuity

Track continuity only for:

- recurring structural tensions
- unresolved assumptions
- long-duration macro shifts
- concepts the user has actually revisited
- themes that improve future judgment

Avoid continuity clutter. A reader should not feel watched or pushed.

## CMS Review

Before publishing, check:

- The signal earns attention.
- The second-order effect is concrete.
- The brief can comfortably say less.
- Recommendations are selective.
- There is no overinterpretation language.
- The piece does not manufacture significance.

## Product Identity

BeyondFleet is not a constant analysis machine.

It should behave like a disciplined editorial desk:

- fewer claims
- fewer surfaced threads
- fewer recommendations
- more careful attention
- more trust through omission

The product wins when readers feel they can ignore more noise.
