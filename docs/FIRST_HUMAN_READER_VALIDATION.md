# First Human Reader Validation

This pass validates BeyondFleet as a reading experience, not a feature set.

The question is:

```text
Does this feel good and trustworthy to read?
```

CMS-ready draft:

```text
lib/content/first-human-reader-validation.ts
```

## Test Brief

Cadence: Monday Macro Reset

Title: The Week Starts With a Patience Test

Reader goal:

- feel calmer
- understand the weekly macro question
- separate facts from interpretation
- hold one uncertainty lightly
- leave with one useful question

Editorial arc:

```text
resilient labor data
-> CPI arrives tomorrow
-> rates remain the swing variable
-> wait before turning confidence into conclusion
```

## Source Grounding

The draft uses only public institutional sources:

- BLS Employment Situation, April 2026
- BLS CPI, March 2026 and April CPI release schedule
- U.S. Treasury Daily Par Yield Curve Rates
- Federal Reserve H.4.1 balance sheet release

Source UX goal:

- increase trust
- stay quiet
- avoid academic clutter
- distinguish factual grounding from BeyondFleet interpretation

## Human Reader Protocol

Use 3 to 5 readers if possible.

Do not ask:

```text
Is the feature good?
```

Ask:

```text
How did this make you feel while reading?
```

## Reader Questions

Ask immediately after reading:

1. How did your body feel while reading: calmer, tense, rushed, bored, or grounded?
2. Where did your attention slow down in a good way?
3. Where did the writing feel too structured or AI-like?
4. Did the source notes help trust, or did they interrupt the reading flow?
5. Did the reflection prompt feel natural or like self-improvement homework?
6. What are you carrying forward after reading?
7. How did you feel after finishing: clearer, pressured, overwhelmed, curious, or neutral?

## Observation Notes

During the session, watch for:

- scrolling speed changes
- rereading moments
- where the reader pauses
- where the reader skips
- visible tension around source notes
- whether they type a reflection without prompting
- whether they describe the brief as calm or heavy

## AI Feeling Audit

Flag the brief if readers describe it as:

- too polished
- too neatly structured
- emotionally flat
- overly explanatory
- academic
- generic
- like a template

Good signs:

- "This made me slow down."
- "I trusted it because it did not overclaim."
- "The source note helped but did not get in the way."
- "The question felt easy to answer."
- "I feel clearer, not pushed."

## Cognitive Load Checks

The brief should not produce:

- urgency
- fear
- pressure to act
- too many simultaneous threads
- over-informed fatigue
- a feeling that the reader is being managed

If readers report fatigue, reduce:

- source-note prominence
- section count shown above the article
- explanatory sentences
- recommendation visibility
- repeated uncertainty language

## Success Criteria

The pass is successful when at least 3 readers independently describe the brief
with language close to:

- calm
- clear
- grounded
- trustworthy
- thoughtful
- easy to continue thinking about

The brief does not need to feel exciting. It needs to feel worth returning to.
